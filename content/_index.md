I work on **multimedia compression**, in particular on **Implicit Neural Representations (INRs) for image compression**, and on multimedia systems more broadly: cloud gaming / remote rendering, video coding and steganography, and deepfake forensics. My scientific tutor is [Prof. Sebastiano Battiato](https://web.dmi.unict.it/docenti/sebastiano.battiato). I collaborate regularly with [Dario Allegra](https://web.dmi.unict.it/docenti/dario.allegra) and the IPLab group.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Catania in 2025 with the thesis [*Implicit Neural Representations for Multimedia Compression*](/publications/#thesis). Alongside research, I work as a freelance research consultant and I am an active open-source / indie developer (mostly Rust and Python) — see [Projects](/projects/).

## Research

Over the last years, my research has focused on reducing the gap between neural and conventional image codecs:

- **INR-based image compression**: fast, compact implicit codecs with bottleneck layers and modulated sinusoidal activations (NIF family), loss-function design and perceptual quality (PaaF).
- **Multimedia systems**: open frameworks for video streaming in cloud gaming, QoE under adverse networks (e.g. FPV drone piloting with ROI coding).
- **Media security and forensics**: AV1 codec-level video steganography, JPEG-AI latents for deepfake source attribution.

Full list with venues and citations: [Publications](/publications/).
