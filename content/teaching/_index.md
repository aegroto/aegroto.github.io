Details about official courses are also listed on the [DMI pages](https://web.dmi.unict.it/). Below is a summary of tutoring, seminars and hands-on tutorials — mostly with public code and material.

## Tutorials

- **Implicit Image Compression: Encoding Pictures with Implicit Neural Representations** — Tutorial at *IEEE ICIP 2024*.
  Source code and notebooks: [icip-2024-inr-images-tutorial](https://github.com/aegroto/icip-2024-inr-images-tutorial) (Python).
  Covers INR fundamentals, NIF-style codecs, training and evaluation.

## Tutoring — University of Catania (DMI)

- **Programming I — Tutoring (C)** — 2023.
  Exercises and material: [unict-tutorato-prog1-2023](https://github.com/aegroto/unict-tutorato-prog1-2023).
- **Saturday Morning Snippets — Rust application example** — 2021.
  Introductory Rust material for DMI students: [rust-example](https://github.com/aegroto/rust-example).

## Seminars

- **Rust for Multimedia** — seminar series, editions 2021 and 2023.
  Repos: [rust_for_multimedia_2021](https://github.com/aegroto/rust_for_multimedia_2021), [rust_for_multimedia_2023](https://github.com/aegroto/rust_for_multimedia_2023).
  Topics: Rust basics for multimedia engineers, bindings (e.g. `rsmpeg` demo: [rsmpeg-vcpkg-demo](https://github.com/aegroto/rsmpeg-vcpkg-demo)), performance and safety.
- **JUnit5 vademecum** — testing mini-guide (Java): [junit5-vademecum](https://github.com/aegroto/junit5-vademecum).

## Supervision

I co-supervise BSc/MSc theses on INR-based coding, multimedia systems and Rust tooling together with the IPLab group. If you are a UNICT student interested in these topics, contact me by [email](/contacts/) with CV and a short proposal.
